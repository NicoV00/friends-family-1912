FONTLOG for Fase Pressure

Basic font information
----------------------
Fase Pressure is a modified version of Anybody, released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its targets use measurements of the open base at wght 900 / wdth 110 plus explicit designer choices: uppercase straight and round widths -2%, narrow +1%, wide -3%, diagonal -1%, and tracking -2 units per side. The supplied warped screenshot could not be measured reliably; no reference-derived geometry is claimed. Every outline comes from the open base. No outline, point or kerning pair was taken from any other font.

Use: display. Scripts: latin.

Files: ttf/FasePressure-Black.ttf, webfonts/FasePressure-Black.woff2, OFL.txt, FONTLOG.txt, fonts.css, README.md
